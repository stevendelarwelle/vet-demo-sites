A Little About Us

After doing pest and wildlife control for fifteen years, Jared and his wife, Steph decided it was time for something new. Jared has always enjoyed doing home remodeling projects for his own home so he went with it and started Elite Builders and Remodeling, LLC.

After six years in the carpentry industry, he invested in a spray foam rig and has since dedicated the majority of his time in the past year to spray foam and blown in insulation.

Both Jared and Steph were born and raised in a small town in Western Wisconsin. As high school sweethearts, they got married, had four children and chose to stay in that same small town that formed who they are today. They stay busy with their four kids and business, but love living this life!
