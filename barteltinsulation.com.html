Our Services

Comprehensive Insulation Solutions

From industrial piping to custom blankets, we deliver tailored insulation systems for every application.

Pipe & Tank Insulation

Industrial pipe insulation and tank/boiler insulation for energy conservation, temperature retention, and freeze protection.

Custom Removable Blankets

Custom sewn removable insulation blankets for valve covers, pump housings, heat exchangers, steam & gas turbines, and extruders.

Ductwork Insulation

Professional ductwork insulation for commercial and industrial HVAC systems to maximize energy efficiency and climate control.

Cold-Storage Insulation

Specialized insulation for refrigerated dairy operations, food processing facilities, and cold-storage environments.

Energy Audits

Industrial energy audits to identify insulation deficiencies, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and cut operational costs.

Noise-Control Panels

Sound absorption blankets and noise-control panel systems for industrial environments requiring acoustic management.

Firestop Systems

Professional firestop installation to maintain fire-rated assemblies and ensure code compliance in commercial and industrial facilities.

In-Plant Maintenance & Material Sales

Ongoing MIC group preventative maintenance programs, plus a large inventory of insulation materials for quick-response supply needs.
