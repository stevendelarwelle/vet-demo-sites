Why Closed Cell?

Benefits of Closed Cell Spray Foam

Closed cell spray foam delivers unmatched performance compared to traditional insulation — here's why it's the smart choice.

⚡

2× Fiberglass Performance

Outperforms traditional pink fiberglass insulation by double the R-value per inch.

💧

Does Not Absorb Water

Closed cell foam resists moisture, protecting your structure from water damage.

🛡️

Air Barrier at 1 Inch

Creates a complete air barrier at just one inch of thickness.

🏗️

Adds Structural Strength

Bonds to surfaces and increases racking strength of walls and roofs.

🐜

Stops Pests & Rodents

Seals entry points to prevent insects and rodents from penetrating your structure.

🍃

Prevents Mold & Mildew

Moisture resistance means no environment for mold or mildew to grow.

🌡️

Reduces Energy Use

Dramatically lower heating and cooling costs with superior thermal performance.

🌬️

Improves Air Quality

Seals out pollutants, allergens, and outside contaminants for healthier indoor air.

🔗

Superior Adhesion

Bonds directly to surfaces for a seamless, gap-free insulation layer.
